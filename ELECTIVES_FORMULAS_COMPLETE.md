## Key Implementation Notes

1. **Weight Normalization**: All weights for each elective sum to 1.00 (100%)

2. **Scoring Formula**:
   ```
   Final Score = (Aptitude Fit × 0.5) + (RIASEC Interest Fit × 0.5)
   
   Where:
   - Aptitude Fit = weighted average of student's 4 aptitude scores
     (verbal ability, mathematical ability, science/spatial ability, logical reasoning)
   - RIASEC Interest Fit = weighted average based on elective's RIASEC alignment
   ```

3. **Level-Based Electives**:
   - **Level 1 (Foundation)**: All base-level electives (Biology 1, Chemistry 1, Physics 1, etc.)
   - **Level 2 (Advanced)**: Prerequisite-based advanced electives (Biology 2, Chemistry 2, etc.)
   - **Recommendation Policy**: Only Level 1 electives recommended to students

4. **RIASEC Mapping**:
   - Electives with high weights in **Investigative, Artistic, Enterprising** interests → recommend for **Academic Track**
   - Electives with high weights in **Realistic, Social, Conventional** interests → recommend for **Technical-Professional Track**

5. **Tracking**: Each elective is assigned a weight-based "personality" that aligns with student aptitudes and interests for better career pathway guidance

## Usage Example

**For a student with these scores:**
- Verbal Ability: 85%
- Mathematical Ability: 78%
- Science/Spatial Ability: 92%
- Logical Reasoning: 88%
- RIASEC: I=4.2, A=3.1, E=3.8, R=2.5, S=3.0, C=2.8

**For Chemistry 1 (weights: Academic 0.28, Logical 0.31, Math 0.19, Science 0.14, Technical 0.08):**
- Aptitude Fit = 0.28×(avg aptitude) + 0.31×(logical) + 0.19×(math) + 0.14×(science) + 0.08×(technical)
- RIASEC Fit = strong alignment with Investigative (4.2) and moderate with Artistic (3.1)
- **Final Score** = (Aptitude Fit × 0.5) + (Interest Fit × 0.5)

This elective would receive a strong recommendation for the Academic Track.
